Skeletal Plan

Spinal instrumentation planning & record

A pre-operative planning and documentation aid for spinal instrumentation — used to lay out a construct, record what was implanted, and produce a clear surgical plan.

  • Implant inventory tracking
  • Barcode (UDI / GTIN) scanning
  • PDF surgical-plan export
  • 22 languages
  • Web, iOS and Android
  • Works fully offline

Skeletal Plan is a documentation and planning aid. It is not a medical device and makes no clinical claims.
